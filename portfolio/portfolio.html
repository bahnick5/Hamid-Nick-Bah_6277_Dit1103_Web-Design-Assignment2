FindHome SL

A Student Accommodation Website for Freetown

Find a safe room near campus.

Problem

  • Students cannot easily see which rooms are available or what they cost.
  • Information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, word of mouth and agents.
  • Rooms often do not match their description, so transport money is wasted.
  • Some students are overcharged or meet fake listings.
  • New students cannot judge how safe or how far an area is.

Proposed solution

A simple, mobile-friendly website that connects students with landlords who have rooms near campus.

  • Search and filter by price, area, distance and facilities
  • Photos, price, description and map for every room
  • Contact the landlord by WhatsApp or phone
  • “Verified” label for checked landlords
  • Short form for landlords to list rooms

Target users

  • Students (main): first-year and returning, aged about 17–28
  • Landlords & agents who want to reach students quickly
  • Parents & guardians who help pay and want safety and trust

Project objectives

  • Save students time and transport money
  • Make prices and locations transparent
  • Build trust through verified landlords
  • Work fast on a phone with limited data
  • Deliver a responsive 7-page site with 4+ interactive features, forms and testing

Who we design for
FindHome SL

Target users

Students in Freetown who rent rooms outside campus. They are young, comfortable with smartphones and social media, have limited budgets and limited mobile data, and many are new to the city.

Method: own experience as a student + 3 questions asked to classmates (how did you find your room? hardest part? what do you want on a website?).

Website structure

  • 7 pages: Home, Find a Room, Room Details, List a Room, About, FAQ, Contact
  • Home has search box, 3 benefit cards and featured rooms
  • Find a Room is the service page: filters + room cards
  • Room Details is the major feature: gallery, facilities, map, contact buttons
  • List a Room is the landlord form (validated)

Navigation

  • Main menu at the top of every page: Home, Find a Room, List a Room, About, FAQ, Contact
  • Mobile: menu collapses into a hamburger icon
  • Logo always returns to Home
  • Footer: quick links, contact details on every page
  • Search box on Home so students start immediately

Technical Development

How it was built
FindHome SL

HTML5

  • Semantic: header, nav, main, section, article, footer
  • Skip link, labels, aria-live error messages
  • 7 pages, shared header and footer
  • Meta description and theme colour on every page

CSS3

  • Mobile-first with breakpoints at 640px and 900px
  • CSS custom properties for the colour system
  • Grid + Flexbox layouts
  • Dark mode via data-theme variables
  • prefers-reduced-motion support

JavaScript: 7 interactive features

  • Live search, filters and sorting
  • Favourites saved in localStorage
  • Room gallery with thumbnails
  • FAQ accordion
  • Hamburger menu
  • Dark / light theme (remembered)
  • Form validation with inline errors + photo preview
